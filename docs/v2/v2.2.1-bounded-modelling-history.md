# V2.2.1 bounded canonical modelling history

## A. Purpose

V2.2.1 builds the trustworthy full-field lap evidence that a future pace estimator may see at historical cutoff `T`. It is data engineering and factual classification only: it computes no representative pace, tyre slope, uncertainty or recommendation. It implements the input boundary designed in [V2.2.0](v2.2-pace-tyre-model.md).

## B. Data flow

`HistoricalRaceStateRequest -> V2.1 RaceState reconstruction -> session-scoped OpenF1 evidence -> adapter history transformation -> ModellingLapObservation[] -> PaceEstimationContext`

`build_historical_pace_context` is the application entry point. It reconstructs canonical state first, then parses the seven modelling feeds and calls the pure `build_pace_context` adapter transformation. OpenF1 DTOs do not cross into the domain context.

## C. Cutoff ownership

The builder never calculates another cutoff. `RaceState.observation_cutoff` is the sole `T`, and `PaceEstimationContext.as_of` equals it. Evidence after `T` is counted by application diagnostics and excluded before alignment.

## D. Lap admission

A lap is admitted only when its provider driver maps to a canonical competitor already in `RaceState`, its lap number is a positive integer, `date_start` is a usable aware timestamp, `lap_duration` is finite and positive, and `date_start + lap_duration <= T`. Completion uses exactly the V2.1 derivation. There is no lap-number, session-end or guessed-duration fallback. Every canonical competitor may contribute its own completed prefix; field symmetry and the focal driver's lap count are not imposed.

## E. Admission rejection, bounded-out and scope filtering

Missing start/duration is `MISSING_TIMING`; a supplied non-positive/non-finite duration is `INVALID_DURATION`; an inadmissible lap number is `INVALID_LAP_NUMBER`; competing usable timing rows for one canonical competitor/lap are `CONFLICTING_TIMING_EVIDENCE`. These prevent admission and are counted by typed reason. A row completed after `T` is bounded out, not malformed. A foreign session is a scope rejection. An unknown provider competitor is diagnosed as unmapped and cannot create a canonical identity.

## F. Canonical identity

The adapter maps the OpenF1 `driver_number` external metadata already attached to each `CarState` to that car's existing `CompetitorId`. It neither calls the canonical identity algorithm again nor emits session keys/driver numbers as model identity.

## G. Duplicate and conflict policy

Exact provider-row duplicates are deterministically deduplicated. Distinct usable lap rows for the same competitor/lap cause that modelling lap to be omitted. Stint rows are deduplicated by full provider shape; differing compound, start-age or stint semantics at the applicable start omit the annotation while retaining valid lap timing. Provider response ordering cannot choose a winner.

## H. Stint and tyre annotation

A stint start is available only when `lap_start <=` that competitor's latest defensibly completed lap at `T`. Starts beyond that prefix are ignored and counted. For a lap, only the latest admitted start at/before that lap applies. Age uses the accepted V2.1 formula: `tyre_age_at_start + lap_number - lap_start + 1`. Negative ages are never clamped; invalid annotations remain absent. Missing compound stays missing, an unrecognised supplied compound maps to `UNKNOWN`, tyre-set identity is not inferred, and provider `lap_end` is never read as historical truth. Untimestamped stint provenance uses `observed_at=None` and explains the bounded-start rule.

## I. Race-control alignment and lap-window exclusion

The V2.1 global reducer is shared directly. It admits explicit supported global/session GREEN, global YELLOW, VSC, SC and RED; ignores driver/sector flags and pit-exit green; does not treat “SC in this lap” or “VSC ending” as GREEN; and resolves same-time state as RED > SC > VSC > YELLOW > GREEN. `TrackStatusEvidence` is the last established state at lap completion.

Factual exclusions also inspect the whole `[date_start, completed_at]` window. A non-green state active at the start or established during the lap yields the matching `SAFETY_CAR`, `VSC`, `RED_FLAG` or `GLOBAL_YELLOW` exclusion even when completion state is green. Reasons are unique and emitted in enum order.

## J. Weather alignment

Each lap receives the latest usable sample with `observed_at <= completed_at`; the latest sample at `T` is never applied backwards. Invalid samples are skipped, no prior sample yields `None`, and there is no interpolation or coefficient. `WEATHER_TRANSITION` is emitted only for a directly observed categorical rainfall-state change within the lap when a prior rainfall state is known.

## K. Gap and position alignment

Per competitor, the builder selects the latest usable interval no later than lap completion, falling back past malformed newer rows. Position at that time supports leader normalization only. Seconds, explicit zero and `+N LAP(S)` preserve canonical `Gap` semantics. P1 has `gap_ahead=None`; an explicit `gap_to_leader=0.0` remains zero. No gap-behind or dirty-air loss is fabricated, and `TRAFFIC_SUSPECTED` remains unset.

## L. Pit evidence

An explicit bounded lap marker yields `PIT_OUT`. A timestamped pit-lane passage yields `PIT_LANE_AFFECTED` only when its provider lap number matches and its timestamp lies inside that exact lap window. Exact duplicates are harmless; future and adjacent passages do not contaminate a lap. Passage is not stationary-stop evidence, tyre-change evidence or pit-stop count.

## M. DataQuality

Every admitted observation has inspectable quality and `completeness=None`. Missing tyre, race-control, weather or gap context is recorded as missing optional evidence, not timing failure or automatic `INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT`. Conflicting stint evidence is a warning. The approximate OpenF1 completion derivation is always disclosed, so current OpenF1 observations are `DEGRADED`, not probabilistic or “low confidence.”

## N. Provenance

Lap timing records the completion derivation. Weather, gap and race-control preserve bounded source timestamps. Stints never receive a fabricated event timestamp. Every source `observed_at` respects its lap/cutoff validator; later `retrieved_at` remains valid. Provider keys remain adapter/provenance metadata only, and future evidence cannot enter canonical provenance.

## O. Deterministic ordering and request behavior

Observations are sorted by canonical `CompetitorId`, then lap number, exactly as the domain contract requires. Inputs are normalized, deduplicated and sorted by timestamp/stable record key, so provider ordering does not affect context or diagnostics. Access is broad and session-scoped: the existing reconstruction performs ten endpoint requests, and the seven repeated modelling feeds use the same `OpenF1Client` keys and are cache hits. There are no per-car/per-lap requests, database or background process.

## P. Prefix stability

For `T1 < T2`, every observation completed by `T1` must be byte-for-model equal in the `T2` prefix. Later laps, stint starts, control events, weather, intervals, position and pits cannot revise earlier timing, annotation, context, exclusions, quality or provenance. Synthetic adversarial tests and the live Monaco/Spain lap-20 versus lap-50 comparisons both produced zero changed prefix observations.

## Q. Builder diagnostics

`ModellingHistoryDiagnostics` reports source/admitted lap counts, typed admission rejection counts, bounded-out and foreign rows, unmapped competitors, exact/conflicting lap evidence, future stint starts, ambiguous annotations, alignment coverage and pit-affected observations. Diagnostics may describe ignored future/provider evidence; none of those counts enter the immutable domain context.

Modern full-race future rows were bounded out without changing lap-20 context. Pit passage timestamps did not satisfy the exact same-lap interval rule in these samples, so no pit-lane-affected classification was invented.

## S. Known limitations

Lap completion timestamps are approximate; stint endpoints are untimestamped and `lap_end` is hindsight-unsafe; tyre-set identity and fuel mass are unavailable; gaps prove proximity rather than dirty-air loss; weather is sampled rather than continuous/local; global control cannot describe every local driving condition; pit passage is not stop count; model-input quality is neither confidence nor probability.

## T. Non-goals

There is no mean/median pace result, regression, degradation slope, fuel/track/traffic correction, uncertainty fit, simulation, public API, frontend change, strategy, V2.3 or V2.4 work.

## U. V2.2.2 boundary

V2.2.2 may consume only `PaceEstimationContext`. It should version a deterministic factual-exclusion selection policy and implement an interpretable representative clean-lap point baseline with fit diagnostics. It must not receive OpenF1 DTOs or recover evidence the builder omitted, and tyre-slope estimation remains separately gated by adequate identifiable age span and confounder review.
