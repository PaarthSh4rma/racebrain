# ADR-0008 — Operator-interface design principles

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The current hero, glass cards, glows and AI emphasis reduce information density and blur evidence hierarchy.

## Decision
The future workbench prioritises dense readable tables/matrices, state visibility, fast comparison, tabular numerals, provenance, explicit warnings and restrained hierarchy in a neutral dark system with one accent. Avoid marketing heroes, dominant glassmorphism, decorative animation, oversized copy, ungrounded AI branding, excessive rounded cards and generic “vibecoded dashboard” aesthetics.

## Consequences
Visual work waits for domain/API capability and is judged by scanability and decision traceability.

## Alternatives Considered
Incremental cosmetic polish to V1. It would preserve the wrong information architecture.

## Migration Impact
Audit now; redesign in V2.5 after StrategyEvaluation and recommendation contracts are served.
