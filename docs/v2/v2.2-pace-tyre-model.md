# V2.2 pace and tyre modelling foundation

## A. Objective

V2.2 will estimate, at a declared historical cutoff, a competitor's representative clean-lap pace, its evolution with tyre age, the uncertainty supported by the evidence, and the evidence behind the result. V2.2.0 establishes vocabulary, bounded model inputs, output contracts and validation methodology. It does not fit or serve a production estimator and makes no strategy recommendation.

## B. Evidence audit matrix

All provider rows must pass through an adapter before model code. “Safe” means the value was observable at or before cutoff `T`; it does not mean that the value identifies a causal effect.

| Input | Source and unit | Temporal semantics / timestamp | Historical safety | Boundary and ambiguity | V2.2 use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lap time | OpenF1 `laps.lap_duration`, seconds | Lap `date_start` plus duration approximates completion | Safe only when derived completion `<= T`; missing/non-positive/non-finite durations are unusable | Canonical `LapObservation`; provider completion is approximate | Primary observed response; V2.2.1 builds longer canonical history |
| Lap number | OpenF1 `laps.lap_number`, count | Associated with the lap row | Safe with a bounded completed lap | Canonical positive integer; not a timestamp and not sufficient alone | Ordering and race-start exclusion |
| Pit-out marker | OpenF1 `laps.is_pit_out_lap`, boolean | Associated with the bounded lap | Safe when the lap is bounded | Direct provider observation mapped to canonical evidence | Hard exclusion when true |
| Pit-lane passage | OpenF1 `pit`, timestamp and lap number | Timestamped passage | Safe only when `date <= T` | Passage is not proof of a stationary stop or total stop count | May establish a pit-lane-affected hard exclusion after alignment |
| Stint / compound / tyre age | OpenF1 `stints`, lap counts and label | Aggregate rows have no event timestamp | `lap_start` is usable only when that start is bounded by the competitor's proven lap at `T`; `lap_end` is never assumed known | Provider-specific input normalized to `TyreCompound`, age and optional stint; set identity is unavailable | Bounded starts may annotate model laps; never ingest future starts or final endpoints |
| Position | OpenF1 `position.position`, ordinal | Timestamped sample | Safe at/before `T` | Canonical snapshot today; position is not pace | Context/diagnostics only; deeper effects later |
| Intervals/gaps | OpenF1 `intervals`, seconds or lapped token | Timestamped sample | Safe at/before `T`, and per-lap use requires alignment no later than lap completion | Canonical `Gap`; proximity is observable, dirty-air loss is not | Optional warning evidence; no traffic coefficient in V2.2 |
| Weather | OpenF1 `weather`: °C, %, m/s, rain flag | Timestamped sample | Safe at/before `T`; latest-at-cutoff must not be applied retrospectively | Canonical aligned `WeatherState`; sparse sampling and local conditions remain unknown | Per-lap context and transition warning in later builder; no coefficient now |
| Race control | OpenF1 `race_control`, event timestamp | Timestamped event stream | Only events `<= T`; per-lap state is reduced using the accepted global reducer through that lap completion | Only global track/session semantics; sector/local flags must not become global exclusions | Align canonical event-derived state per lap; known SC/VSC/red/global yellow are hard exclusions |
| Data quality | Adapter/reconstructor | Describes only evidence admitted by `T` | Must itself be cutoff-bounded | Not confidence or probability | Propagated into inputs, estimates and diagnostics |
| Provenance | Adapter/reconstructor | Carries observed/retrieved times and transformations | No post-cutoff observation may be included | Provider identifiers are metadata only | Required audit trail, never model identity |
| Fuel mass | Not supplied by current feeds | Unavailable | Cannot be reconstructed defensibly | Major pace confounder | Excluded from V2.2.0; future explicit configured or jointly estimated treatment |
| Track evolution | Not directly measured as a single variable | Latent/common-mode race-time process | Cannot be assigned from an individual lap | Confounded with fuel and tyre age | Future field-wide/common-mode study, not a hard-coded correction |

V1 `TYRE_BASE_PACE`, `TYRE_DEGRADATION`, linear race engine and Monte Carlo code were inspected only as legacy lessons. Their soft/medium/hard constants, noise, “confidence” and strategy-frequency semantics are not V2 evidence and are not imported.

## C. Historical/no-hindsight policy

For an estimate as of `T`, every admitted modelling lap must have a derived completion at or before `T`. Its exclusion decision, tyre annotation, aligned global status, weather, gap evidence, quality and provenance must be derived solely from source observations no later than that lap completion and `T`. `Provenance.observed_at` is therefore bounded to the owning lap or estimate cutoff, including provenance nested in aligned evidence. `retrieved_at` is deliberately not bounded: an archived historical observation may be fetched long after the race without becoming hindsight. Untimed records cannot affect a model.

OpenF1 stint rows are untimestamped aggregates. A stint start is admitted only when `lap_start <=` that competitor's latest proven lap at `T`. If a subsequent admitted start exists, it bounds the previous stint to the lap before that start. A later start beyond the competitor's state at `T` and every provider `lap_end` are ignored. Consequently the current stint may remain open and compound/age may remain missing. Full-session final stint information must never repair a historical context.

`RaceState` remains the canonical bounded snapshot. `PaceEstimationContext` combines that snapshot with a longer, immutable sequence of provider-independent `ModellingLapObservation` values. It enforces the identical cutoff, canonical competitors, unique deterministic competitor/lap ordering, and completion `<= T`. OpenF1 DTOs and provider keys cannot enter this boundary.

Future data belongs only to an evaluator after prediction and inputs are frozen. It may score an estimate but may not alter inputs, classification, fitting, uncertainty, model selection or data quality.

All accepted timezone-aware timestamps on new V2.2 contracts are stored in UTC, including context/estimate cutoffs, evidence-window boundaries, lap completions, diagnostic cutoffs and aligned-evidence observation times. Naive datetimes are invalid.

## D. Pace definition

- **Observed lap time** is a measured completed-lap duration in seconds, for example `81.324 s`.
- **Representative clean-lap pace** is an estimator's expected lap duration under its declared clean-lap selection and context, expressed in `s/lap`, for example `81.15 s/lap`. It is not an observed lap and its value must be finite and strictly positive.
- **Relative pace** is conceptually an estimated signed difference in `s/lap` from a named reference evaluated at the same declared cutoff and compatible context. Positive means slower. V2.2.0 does not serialize relative `PaceEstimate` output: a free-text reference cannot prove cohort, subject, cutoff or compatible context, and the typed reference policy is deferred until V2.2.2 selects a real baseline.
- **Tyre-age slope** is a change in lap-time contribution per additional lap of tyre age. It is not called pace delta.

The V2.2.0 `PaceEstimate` represents only representative clean-lap pace and declares subject, `as_of`, samples, evidence window, model metadata, data quality, limitations and optional uncertainty. `TriggerMetric.PACE_DELTA_S_PER_LAP` remains unchanged for later work.

## E. Tyre degradation definition

`TyreDegradationEstimate.value`, in `s/lap/lap`, is the estimated tyre-age pace slope: the change in fitted lap time, in seconds, associated with one additional lap of tyre age under the estimator's declared assumptions. Thus `0.041 s/lap/lap` means approximately 0.041 seconds slower on the next lap per additional tyre-age lap. It is not claimed to be pure physical tyre degradation because fuel burn, track evolution, driver behaviour, traffic, weather and race control may not be separately identified. The existing `DegradationEstimate` name remains an alias. The contract includes competitor, compound, optional stint, cutoff, sample count and a strictly positive tyre-age span (`maximum > minimum`); the signed slope remains unconstrained.

## F. Clean-lap taxonomy

The taxonomy is closed and separates three stages. `LapAdmissionRejectionReason` records provider rows rejected before a canonical modelling observation exists: `MISSING_TIMING` and `INVALID_DURATION`. The V2.2.1 history builder will count/report these in admission diagnostics and must not fabricate a `ModellingLapObservation` for them. `LapExclusionReason` applies only after valid timing and admission, and soft warnings remain distinct from both.

| Hard exclusion | Defensible meaning |
|---|---|
| `LAP_ONE` | Race-start effects make lap one unlike steady running |
| `PIT_OUT` | Bounded lap row explicitly marks pit out |
| `PIT_LANE_AFFECTED` | Bounded timestamped pit passage has been aligned to the lap |
| `SAFETY_CAR` / `VSC` | Accepted global reducer establishes the status for the lap |
| `RED_FLAG` | Accepted global reducer establishes suspended/red context |
| `GLOBAL_YELLOW` | Accepted reducer establishes a track-wide yellow; local/sector flags do not qualify |
| `INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT` | Required context for a specific configured fit is absent; the missing requirement must be reported |

Soft warnings are `WEATHER_TRANSITION`, `TRAFFIC_SUSPECTED`, and `EXTREME_OUTLIER`. They describe a rule's suspicion and must carry evidence/diagnostics; they are not automatic exclusions in the foundation and do not prove rain causality, dirty air, or invalid driving. Exact detection rules belong to versioned model configuration and V2.2.1 implementation.

## G. Confounders

- **Fuel:** no trustworthy per-lap mass exists in the current pipeline. No fuel load or magic correction is invented.
- **Track evolution:** common race-time improvements can oppose apparent degradation. Individual stint slope cannot identify the two effects.
- **Traffic:** a bounded gap proves proximity only. It cannot establish aerodynamic loss or a seconds-per-lap penalty; deeper traffic modelling belongs to V2.3.
- **Weather:** samples must be aligned historically per lap. Latest weather at `T` cannot describe earlier laps, and no weather coefficient is selected here.
- **Race control:** a single cutoff snapshot is insufficient for historical fitting. A future builder must replay the same approved global reducer to each lap completion and must ignore local/sector flags as global state.

## H. Proposed model input flow

`bounded provider rows -> tolerant OpenF1 DTOs -> canonical history builder -> ModellingLapObservation[] + RaceState -> PaceEstimationContext -> deterministic estimator`

The history builder belongs in the application/adapter boundary, not in the estimator. It will align weather, global status and optional proximity using only evidence available by each lap and `T`, apply the stint-start rule, attach quality/provenance, and produce deterministic ordering. It does not create a second `RaceState`.

## I. Estimate/output contracts

Existing `ModelEstimate`, `ModelVersion`, `Uncertainty`, `StatisticalInterval`, `EmpiricalQuantiles`, `DataQuality` and `Provenance` are reused. `PaceEstimate` and `DegradationEstimate` are refined with modelling-specific semantics. `EvidenceWindow` makes lap/time extent explicit. In `FitDiagnostics`, `candidate_laps` means valid admitted observations considered for fitting, `included_laps` means candidates included in the fit, and `excluded_laps` means admitted candidates removed by clean-lap rules; the latter two must reconcile to candidates. Malformed or missing-timing provider rows are admission diagnostics for V2.2.1 and never candidate laps. No public API is added.

## J. Uncertainty semantics

An estimate may have no uncertainty; missing spread is not zero. Allowed initial representations remain exactly one of: an explicitly described bounded/statistical interval, empirical P10/P50/P90 quantiles with sample count, or a normal standard deviation only when a future estimator genuinely justifies that assumption. The intended first baseline should prefer point-only or residual/bootstrap empirical intervals. Any resampling seed must be typed and explicit. Model confidence, data quality and scenario frequency are distinct from probability; no percentage “confidence” is produced.

## K. Config/versioning

There is no model registry. `PaceModelConfig` is strict, frozen, version-controlled and inspectable. Canonical JSON with sorted keys and compact separators is SHA-256 hashed; identical typed configuration produces identical `model_name`, `version` and `config_hash`, and a changed field changes the hash. `random_seed` is optional now and mandatory if a later uncertainty method is stochastic. With identical canonical evidence, cutoff, configuration and model version, the estimator must be deterministic.

## L. Validation design

Random lap splits are forbidden. Use event-grouped chronological rolling-origin evaluation for within-event updating and forward-event validation for generalisation. At each origin, training/context ends at the prediction cutoff; later laps are evaluator-only. Freeze prediction, metadata and input hashes before loading outcomes.

Metrics are adopted only where sample support makes them meaningful: lap-time MAE, RMSE, median absolute error and residual bias; residual distributions by tyre age; tyre-age slope error/stability; empirical interval coverage and width; estimate availability/sample coverage; errors segmented by compound and by defensibly aligned track-status/weather regime. Report missingness and segment counts. Counterfactual strategy metrics, causal traffic loss and arbitrary dashboard targets are excluded.

The intended first numerical baseline is deliberately interpretable: robust/median clean-lap summaries for representative pace and a low-order robust slope against tyre age, initially without pretending fuel and track evolution are separated. A later accepted design may test field-wide common-mode adjustment. Residual or seeded bootstrap empirical intervals may follow only after baseline residual behaviour is measured. No neural network, LLM or complex ML dependency is justified.

## M. Live OpenF1 audit and known limitations

Audited 10 September 2026 against OpenF1 session 9523 (Monaco 2024, focal car 16) and 9539 (Spain 2024, focal car 4). Counts below use derived completed-lap cutoffs; “history” is completed timed laps per car and may differ by one from the focal decision-lap label.

| Session / focal cutoff | UTC cutoff | Bounded timed laps | Per-car availability | Bounded stint starts / later starts rejected | Weather / race-control / interval / pit rows available by cutoff |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Monaco lap 20 | 14:11:16.500 | 289 | 16 cars; 18–19, median 18; focal 19 | 33 / 6 | 123 / 49 / 6,228 / 17 |
| Monaco lap 50 | 14:50:36.056 | 763 | 16 cars; 47–49, median 48; focal 49 | 35 / 4 | 162 / 70 / 15,535 / 19 |
| Spain lap 20 | 13:30:17.898 | 380 | 20 cars; 18–20, median 19; focal 20 | 36 / 26 | 84 / 29 / 7,763 / 17 |
| Spain lap 50 | 14:10:47.535 | 977 | 20 cars; 47–50, median 49; focal 50 | 61 / 1 | 125 / 71 / 19,557 / 42 |

At Monaco lap 20 the bounded starts showed 18 hard and 15 medium records; at lap 50 they showed 19 hard, 15 medium and 1 soft. At Spain lap 20 they showed 14 medium and 22 soft; at lap 50, 13 hard, 19 medium and 29 soft. All admitted rows in these samples supplied non-negative `tyre_age_at_start`, but this does not make the provider endpoints historically knowable.

Provider quirks: Monaco had 20 lap rows without a usable positive duration and Spain had one such row (also missing `date_start`). Monaco exposed `lap_end` in 39/43 stint rows and Spain in 62/62, yet all stint rows were untimestamped, so those endpoints are hindsight-unsafe. Both sessions had timestamped weather/control samples, but sample frequency differs and alignment is still required. Intervals are dense but are proximity evidence, not a causal traffic penalty. Monaco's red-flag/pathological race shape makes final stint aggregates especially misleading. Other unavailable truths include fuel mass, precise tyre set identity/condition, local weather, driver instructions, energy deployment and physical clean-air potential.

## N. V2.2.0 non-goals

No production estimator, fitted coefficient, pace/degradation endpoint, placeholder response, strategy or recommendation logic, V2.3 traffic model, V2.4 strategy work, frontend pace columns, visual redesign, pandas/scikit-learn dependency, model registry or large raw provider dump is included. V1 runtime and `/workbench` remain unchanged.

## O. Proposed delivery sequence

1. **V2.2.1 — bounded modelling evidence builder:** create provider-independent full-history observations using the stint-start policy and per-lap weather/global-status/gap alignment; add adversarial future-contamination and cutoff-advance tests.
2. **V2.2.2 — representative pace baseline:** implement deterministic clean-lap classification and an interpretable point estimator with diagnostics; establish chronological baseline results before thresholds.
3. **V2.2.3 — tyre-age slope and empirical uncertainty:** fit the interpretable slope, quantify confounding, add deterministic residual/bootstrap intervals where justified, and run grouped rolling-origin/forward-event validation. Stable API/UI exposure follows semantic and FDE acceptance, not the foundation.
